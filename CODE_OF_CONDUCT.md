# Code of Conduct

Kubereum follows the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) for all open source projects, issue trackers, discussions, and direct community interactions.

## In short

We expect everyone interacting with our repositories, issues, pull requests, and community spaces to:

- Be respectful and patient
- Assume good intent
- Disagree with ideas, not people
- Give credit where it is due
- Keep technical discussions technical

## Reporting

If someone violates this code, email conduct@kubereum.com. The team will review the report, follow up with the people involved, and take action when needed.

Reports are confidential. We do not share names without consent unless required to keep someone safe or by law.

## Enforcement

Depending on the situation, action can range from a warning, a temporary ban from a repository or community space, to a permanent ban. We aim to be fair and proportionate.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant. The full text is available at the link above.

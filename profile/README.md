# Kubereum

**Software for modern operations. Engineered in India.**

Kubereum Private Limited builds tools that help modern teams run their business — CRM, HRM, billing, and operations software, designed for Indian companies but built to global standards.

## What we work on

- **CRM** — sales and customer relationship workflows for India-first teams
- **HRM** — payroll, attendance, leave, and people operations
- **Billing** — GST-compliant invoicing, recurring revenue, and finance ops
- **Operations platform** — workflows, integrations, and the connective tissue underneath

## Engineering

We are a small founder-led team. We build with TypeScript, Postgres, and the boring parts of the cloud. We optimise for clarity, reliability, and long-term maintainability over short-term cleverness.

The website you are reading is deployed on Cloudflare Pages. Performance, accessibility, and SEO are first-class concerns, not afterthoughts.
